# Surface brief — Home (and site shell)

Scope: home route plus shared shell (nav, footer, type, tokens). Visitor mode: Persuade (Experience on the Charuto chapters).
Audience: classic Mercedes collectors browsing on phones at night; owners considering restoration. Actions: open a WhatsApp conversation about a specific car; request a restoration evaluation (optionally from a Vision concept).
Proof on hand: one real photograph of the W111 "Charuto"; one AI concept image (illustration only). No other real photos — placeholders must say so.
Constraints: brief-pinned palette, serif display + sans UI, pinned hero copy, no gold excess, no invented facts/prices/contacts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: MERCANTIL is a living factory brochure for one collection — every car is a spread, and the period paint-and-upholstery sample chart is the control system. It refuses the dealership grid and the gold-on-black luxury hero.

OWN-WORLD: Night plates (near-black #090A0B, navy #101A28) carry photography; ivory paper pages (#E8E1D5) carry text and specification. The rectangular colour sample chip with its printed name is the single control unit: filters, Vision presets, stage markers. Bodoni Moda at brochure scale, Archivo for captions and specs, silver hairlines. Wine (#562C32 family) is the only action tint. Every image carries its provenance caption.

STORY: The visitor enters a collection at night, meets the Charuto chapter by chapter, browses the collection by model and decade, sees how restoration is approached, imagines their own car in Vision, then talks to Mercantil on WhatsApp or requests an evaluation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed night plate; the Charuto (concept illustration) emerges from darkness under a moving light; MERCANTIL in Bodoni spans the width behind the car roof (real alpha matte from the image); copy column bottom-left: "Clássicos com história." + subtitle + primary "Explorar acervo" (wine) and secondary "Restaurar meu carro"; provenance caption bottom-left. (Amended from "right column": the car fills the right two-thirds of the only concept image, so the plate is shifted right and copy sits in the freed left column instead of covering the grille and the matte focal point.)
