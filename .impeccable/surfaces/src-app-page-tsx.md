# Surface: Downtown Burger home (/)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a hungry person, usually on a phone, who should want the burger and order it within a minute.
Direction: pinned by the brief (Downtown's own menu identity + reference video's colour-scene choreography). Code-led (no image generation).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The burger is the hero. One rendered Downtown burger (The Bacon Street) lives on a pinned stage while five saturated scene fields wipe upward beneath it like diner signage changing colour. The burger splits across each wipe and re-stacks. Refuses: the split hero with a photo on the right and a card grid below.

OWN-WORLD: The menu's own world, made saturated. Scene fields: Downtown red, burnt orange, diner green, burgundy, diner teal. Cream (#F2E8D5) and charcoal (#1C1814) carry the commerce. Mustard is the single action colour. Display face: Big Shoulders Display, heavy and condensed, Chicago skyline lettering. Body face: Barlow. Five-point stars as the only ornament. Rules borrowed from the menu's double-line frame. Ghost outline wordmark behind the burger.

STORY: See the burger → want it (flavour, layers) → choose (signature four) → understand it (exploded, labelled, true ingredients) → order (real menu, cart, WhatsApp).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed red field. Ghost "DOWNTOWN" outline across the full width behind. Centre: the burger at about 46vh, floating over a soft floor shadow.
