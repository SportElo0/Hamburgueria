1. What this agreement covers

1.1 Growlift will design and build a website for the Client's business and keep it online (hosting), with small updates and support, as described below. The Client's written proposal (the "Proposal") forms part of this agreement. If they conflict, this agreement prevails.

1.2 This agreement is between businesses. The Client confirms it is acting for its business and not as a consumer.

2. Services

2.1 Website. A website made from scratch for the Client's business, designed for phones first, with the Client's photos, texts, menu or services, opening hours and location, a WhatsApp button and a map.

2.2 Google Business Profile. Growlift sets up or updates the Client's Google Business Profile (Google Maps listing). Google verifies business profiles and may require action from the Client. Growlift does not guarantee approval, timing, or any position in Google results.

2.3 Hosting, small updates and support, while the monthly plan is active:

  • keeping the website online on the Client's domain;
  • small updates on request, such as changing prices, opening hours, photos, menu or service items and short texts;
  • support by WhatsApp or e-mail, in English, on Brazilian business days.

2.4 Not included unless agreed in writing: the domain registration fee; new pages or features beyond the Proposal; a full redesign; online ordering or payment systems; photo or video production; paid advertising; and third-party tools or subscriptions.

If the amount is still unpaid 15 days after the due date, Growlift may pause hosting and updates (and take the website offline) after 5 days' written notice, until the amount is paid. Amounts due stay due while the website is paused. Growlift charges no late-payment interest or fee.

3.5 Price. The US$ 40 installments and the US$ 47 monthly fee do not change during the first 12 months. After that, the monthly fee stays at US$ 47 unless both parties agree in writing to a different amount.

4. Delivery

4.1 The Client sends its material: photos, texts, and menu or services (and its logo, if it has one). The Client confirms it has the right to use all material it sends.

4.2 The website goes live within 7 Brazilian business days after Growlift receives all the material listed in clause 4.1. Google Business Profile verification is done by Google and is not part of this deadline.

4.3 Before going live, the Client may ask for changes to the design and content within the scope of the Proposal. Growlift then publishes the website on the Client's domain.

5. Term and cancellation

5.1 Start. This agreement starts when the Client signs it (or confirms acceptance in writing) and makes the first payment. It continues month by month until cancelled.

5.2 First 7 days. The Client may cancel within 7 calendar days after accepting the Proposal, with no fee, and Growlift refunds everything the Client paid.

5.3 After 7 days. The Client may cancel at any time by writing to contato@growlift.com.br or on WhatsApp. The cancellation applies from the first monthly charge date after Growlift receives the request: the US$ 47 fee is not charged from that date. The Client chooses one of these options:

  1. Keep the website: the Client keeps paying the remaining website installments (US$ 40) on the same monthly dates, Growlift hands over the website files within 10 business days after the Client chooses, and the hosting, updates and support (US$ 47 per month) end.
  2. Do not keep the website: Growlift takes the website offline, the Client owes nothing more, and payments already made are not refunded.

5.4 If the Client has not chosen option (a) or (b) within 15 days after cancelling, Growlift will send a written reminder by e-mail and WhatsApp that says clearly: "If you do not choose by [date], we will take your website offline and you will not receive the files." If the Client still has not chosen 10 days after the reminder, option (b) applies. Until the Client chooses, Growlift keeps the website online and charges no further payment. If the Client chooses option (a), the installments that fell due during this period are charged together on the next monthly date.

5.5 After the 12 installments. Once all 12 website installments are paid, the Client may cancel the monthly plan at any time, keep the website and receive the files. There is nothing more to pay.

5.6 Ending by Growlift. Growlift may end this agreement immediately by written notice if (a) the Client does not pay an amount within 30 days after its due date and still does not pay within 10 days after a written reminder, or (b) the Client uses the website for unlawful content and does not remove it within 5 days after written notice. In these cases, the Client may choose option (a) in clause 5.3 after paying any overdue amount. Growlift may also end this agreement for any other reason with 30 days' written notice. In that case, no payment is due after the end date, Growlift hands over the website files within 10 business days, and the website becomes the Client's property without the remaining website installments being due. If the website has not yet gone live, Growlift refunds everything the Client paid.

6. Ownership and content

6.1 The domain belongs to the Client from the start. Growlift gives the Client access to it, or its transfer code, whenever the Client asks.

6.2 Ownership of the website. Once all 12 website installments are paid, Growlift assigns to the Client, worldwide, for the full period of legal protection and for all forms of use, all economic rights in the website as delivered (design, texts and images created by Growlift, and the website files), and the website becomes the Client's property. Growlift keeps its general tools, code libraries and templates used in other projects, and grants the Client a perpetual, worldwide licence to use them as included in the website. Third-party components (such as fonts or open-source code) stay under their own licences. Until all 12 installments are paid, Growlift grants the Client a licence to use the website while payments are up to date. If the Client receives the files early under clause 5.3(a), this licence continues and ownership passes when the last installment is paid.

6.3 The Client's photos, texts, logo and brand always remain the Client's. The Client allows Growlift to use them only to build and run the website.

6.4 Hosting, updates, support and any reports or analytics stop when the monthly plan ends.

6.5 Growlift will only show the Client's website in its own portfolio or advertising with the Client's written permission.

7.2 The Client is responsible for the accuracy of the information it provides (such as prices, opening hours and allergen information) and for complying with the rules that apply to its business in its country.

7.3 Neither party is liable for indirect losses, such as lost profits or lost business. Growlift's total liability under this agreement is limited to the amounts the Client paid in the 12 months before the claim. This limit does not apply to refunds under clause 5.2, to wilful misconduct, gross negligence or fraud, or to anything else that cannot be limited by law.

7.4 Neither party is responsible for delays caused by events outside its reasonable control.
