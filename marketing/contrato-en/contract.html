1. What this agreement covers

1.1 Growlift will design and build a website for the Client's business and keep it online (hosting), with small updates and support, as described below. The Client's written proposal (the "Proposal") forms part of this agreement. If they conflict, this agreement prevails.

1.2 This agreement is between businesses. The Client confirms it is acting for its business and not as a consumer.

2. Services

2.1 Website. A website made from scratch for the Client's business, designed for phones first, with the Client's photos, texts, menu or services, opening hours and location, a WhatsApp button and a map.

2.2 Google Business Profile. Growlift sets up or updates the Client's Google Business Profile (Google Maps listing). Google verifies business profiles and may require action from the Client. Growlift does not guarantee approval, timing, or any position in Google results.

2.3 Hosting, small updates and support, while the monthly plan is active:

  • keeping the website online on the Client's domain;
  • small updates on request, such as changing prices, opening hours, photos, menu or service items and short texts;
  • support by WhatsApp or e-mail, in English, on Brazilian business days.

2.4 Not included unless agreed in writing: the domain registration fee; new pages or features beyond the Proposal; a full redesign; online ordering or payment systems; photo or video production; paid advertising; and third-party tools or subscriptions.

3.1 Domain. The domain registration fee is not included. Growlift tells the Client its cost in writing before the Client accepts. The domain is registered in the Client's business name.

3.2 How payment works. Payment is by card, through a secure payment link from Stripe, the payment provider. The first payment of US$ 87 is charged when the Client accepts the Proposal, before work starts. The next payments are charged automatically every month on the same date.

3.3 Taxes and fees. Prices are in US dollars. Taxes, bank charges or currency-conversion fees charged to the Client by its own bank or government are not included and are paid by the Client.

3.4 Failed payments. If a monthly payment fails, Growlift will tell the Client and try again. If it is still unpaid 15 days after the due date, Growlift may pause hosting and updates until it is paid.

3.5 Price changes. The US$ 40 installments do not change. Growlift may change the US$ 47 monthly fee only after the first 12 months, with at least 30 days' written notice. The Client may cancel before the new price applies.

4. Delivery

4.1 The Client sends its material: photos, texts, logo and menu or services. The Client confirms it has the right to use all material it sends.

4.2 The website goes live within 7 Brazilian business days after Growlift receives all the material. Google Business Profile verification is done by Google and is not part of this deadline.

4.3 Before going live, the Client may ask for changes to the design and content within the scope of the Proposal. Growlift then publishes the website on the Client's domain.

5. Term and cancellation

5.1 Start. This agreement starts when the Client accepts the Proposal and pays the first payment. It continues month by month until cancelled.

5.2 First 7 days. The Client may cancel within 7 days after accepting the Proposal, with no fee, and Growlift refunds everything the Client paid.

5.3 After 7 days. The Client may cancel at any time by writing to contato@growlift.com.br or on WhatsApp. The cancellation applies from the next monthly charge. The Client chooses one of these options:

  1. Keep the website: the Client keeps paying the remaining website installments (US$ 40) on the same monthly dates, Growlift hands over the website files, and the hosting, updates and support (US$ 47 per month) end.
  2. Do not keep the website: Growlift takes the website offline, the Client owes nothing more, and payments already made are not refunded.

5.4 If the Client does not choose within 15 days after cancelling, option (b) applies.

5.5 After the 12 installments. Once all 12 website installments are paid, the Client may cancel the monthly plan at any time, keep the website and receive the files. There is nothing more to pay.

5.6 Growlift may end this agreement with 30 days' written notice, or immediately if the Client fails to pay as described in clause 3.4 for more than 30 days or uses the website for unlawful content. In either case, the Client may choose option (a) in clause 5.3.

6. Ownership and content

6.1 The domain belongs to the Client from the start.

6.2 The website becomes the Client's property once all 12 website installments are paid. Until then, Growlift grants the Client the right to use it while payments are up to date.

6.3 The Client's photos, texts, logo and brand always remain the Client's. The Client allows Growlift to use them only to build and run the website.

6.4 Hosting, updates, support and any reports or analytics stop when the monthly plan ends.

6.5 Growlift will only show the Client's website in its own portfolio or advertising with the Client's written permission.

7. Responsibilities

7.1 Growlift will do the work with care and skill. It aims to keep the website online at all times but does not guarantee it will never be unavailable, for example during hosting-provider outages or maintenance.

7.2 The Client is responsible for the accuracy of the information it provides (such as prices, opening hours and allergen information) and for complying with the rules that apply to its business in its country.

7.3 Neither party is liable for indirect losses, such as lost profits or lost business. Growlift's total liability under this agreement is limited to the amounts the Client paid in the 12 months before the claim. This limit does not apply to fraud or to anything that cannot be limited by law.

7.4 Neither party is responsible for delays caused by events outside its reasonable control.
