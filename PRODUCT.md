## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + GSAP/ScrollTrigger, as pinned by the brief.
Menu content lives in structured TypeScript data (`src/content/`), ready for a future CMS.

## Users

- Hungry locals deciding what to order, mostly on a phone, often already knowing they want a burger _(inferred)_.
- First-time visitors who found the brand and want to understand what it is and how good it looks.

## Product Purpose

Downtown Burger is a real Brazilian artisanal burger house. The site exists to make the food irresistible
and then get an order out of the visitor quickly: browse the real menu, customise, build a cart and hand the
order to the restaurant through WhatsApp.

## Positioning

"Burguer artesanal feito com qualidade." A vintage American diner/burger-house identity (skyline badge,
condensed type, cream/charcoal/red, stars) delivered as a premium interactive experience.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Ordering is a frontend cart with a WhatsApp handoff. There's no backend, payment or delivery logistics.
- Prices are stored as integer centavos (BRL).
- Little Downtown includes fries and a juice (grape or orange) at no extra charge.
- Milkshake requires one flavour: morango, Nutella or baunilha.
- Add-ons ("Adicionais") apply to burgers.
- Alcoholic long necks require a legal notice; no real age verification exists.
- Undecided: WhatsApp number, address, opening hours, maps link, Instagram handle, CNPJ, delivery vs pickup.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: DOWNTOWN BURGER. Tagline: "BURGUER ARTESANAL FEITO COM QUALIDADE."
- Badge logo: skyline illustration in a circle, arc text "BURGERS • STEAKS • FRIES • SHAKES".
- Copy primarily in Brazilian Portuguese.

## Evidence on Hand

- Paper menu photo (all products, descriptions, prices).
- No official logo vector, no product photography, no reviews, no history, no address.
- Do not fabricate any of these. Product imagery is an illustrative 3D render, labelled as such.

## Product Principles

1. The menu is the truth: names, descriptions and prices exactly as printed.
2. Desire first, then speed: the cinematic story never blocks ordering.
3. Honest placeholders over invented facts.
4. Every button works.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA target: keyboard-operable overlays, visible focus, reduced-motion alternative, 44px touch targets.
